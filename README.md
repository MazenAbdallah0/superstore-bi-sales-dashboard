# 📊 Superstore BI Sales Dashboard

An executive, high-end Dark-Themed Power BI Dashboard designed for analyzing retail sales performance, profitability margins, total order volumes, and regional distributions.

---

## 🎨 Dashboard Overview & Design System

![Superstore Dashboard](Screenshot%20(1419).png)

### 🖌️ UI/UX & Color Palette
* **Canvas Background:** Dark Navy (`#03142A`)
* **Visual Containers:** Slate Dark (`#1E293B`)
* **Primary Accents:** Electric Blue (`#38BDF8`)

---

## 🔑 Key KPIs & Analytical Insights
* **Total Sales & Profit Tracking:** Comprehensive metrics evaluating total revenue alongside overall profit margins.
* **Order & Quantity Volume:** Tracking total orders fulfilled and total units sold.
* **Regional & Category Breakdown:** Comparative performance across regions and product sub-categories.
* **Yearly Trends:** Time-series trend analysis of total sales over time.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Power BI Desktop:** Custom canvas dimensions, visual hierarchy, dark theme design, and interactive slicers.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Calculated core KPI measures and metrics.
* **Power Query:** Data transformation and cleaning routines.
